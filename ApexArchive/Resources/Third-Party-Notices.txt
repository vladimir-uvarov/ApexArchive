## Photographs

Photos are retrieved from Wikimedia Commons at runtime, subject to metadata checks. Each displayed photo has a credit control linking its creator, license and original file page. Accepted metadata identifies CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, public-domain material, or OGL 3. Named licences must match their recognized URLs; NC and ND licences are rejected. Public-domain items without a separate licence link point to the Commons file’s rights statement. Supplied attribution, credit, copyright and restrictions are retained alongside author and source links. Metadata is evidence, not an independent guarantee of worldwide rights clearance.

Photo rights remain with the credited creators. Display resizing/cropping is disclosed. If a change creates an adaptation of a share-alike photograph, the applicable share-alike terms continue to apply. No third-party driver/car photographs are checked into this repository. If adding marketing screenshots containing photos, preserve the corresponding attribution and license notices.

## Editorial sources and icon

Personal garage entries and track preferences link their individual sources and identify the evidence date. They use short original factual summaries, not copied articles or source photographs. Links alone are not a license for additional reuse.

The approved icon is AI-generated painted helmet artwork, subsequently resized to 1024 × 1024. Its visual similarity to recognizable racing designs remains a separate branding consideration. No athlete, team, Formula 1 organization or data provider endorses this app.
