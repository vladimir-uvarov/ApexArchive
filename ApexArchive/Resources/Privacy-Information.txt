ApexArchive — Privacy information
Operator: Vladimir Uvarov
Updated: 15 September 2026

Current app
ApexArchive has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or subscription. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos-library access, or an advertising identifier. Quiz scores exist only for the current session. The app does not send favorites or quiz answers to the developer.

On-device information
Saved driver identifiers are stored in app preferences. Downloaded archive data, images and their attribution are cached in the app’s private storage. Photo caching has a storage budget and evicts older files; the operating system can also remove caches. The operating system remembers the selected app icon. File timestamps are read only to manage this app’s cache. Removing the app removes its local container; system backups and the system’s icon records are controlled by iOS and your Apple settings.

Network requests
The app contacts GitHub to obtain F1DB releases and Wikimedia services (Wikipedia, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons) to resolve and download images. These services receive normal network information, including your IP address, request time, requested resource and the app’s identifying User-Agent. Image lookups include article titles and may reveal which content is displayed or preloaded. The developer does not operate a server receiving these requests. Providers process connection information under their own policies; their retention, infrastructure and international transfers are not controlled by this app.

External links
Source, licence and support links open external services. Their policies apply when you visit them. Sending a GitHub issue or contacting the operator shares whatever information you choose to submit; GitHub issues are public. Do not include sensitive information in an issue.

Your choices and contact
Favorites can be removed in the app. You can use already downloaded content offline and remove the app to clear local app storage. No developer-held account exists to delete. For privacy questions or rights requests, the current operator contact route is the personal GitHub profile: https://github.com/vladimir-uvarov. A direct privacy/support contact and applicable operator legal details must be finalized before App Store submission.

Future paid features
No payment is collected by this version. If purchases, subscriptions, accounts or analytics are introduced, this information and the App Store privacy disclosures must be reviewed before those features launch.

Museum maps use Apple Maps to search for the selected museum and display map content. This feature does not request your device location. Opening directions leaves the app for Apple Maps, whose own privacy settings apply.
